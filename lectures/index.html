The series

One lecture a year

The LEAP Lecture brings a leading scholar of economic history to Stellenbosch each year, to speak to students, colleagues and the public. The first, in December 2016, was given by Marianne Wanamaker. In 2020 the lecture became an online roundtable, and in 2025 it closed a conference on AI and economic history.
